# New Technician Cheat Sheet (unofficial)

Put together by a technician for the 2025 new starter group. Not reviewed. Use the approved procedures.

## Greasing
- All our drive motors get greased about every three or four months.
- Any NLGI 2 lithium grease is fine; use whatever cartridge is on the shelf.
- About a couple of pumps each end.

## Thermal trips
- Let it cool a bit, reset, see if it holds.

## Who to call
- HVAC: facilities.
- Motors: the maintenance lead.
