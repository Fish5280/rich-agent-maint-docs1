# Toledo Facility: HVAC Zones

Skyline Plastics, Toledo OH (plant id PLT-TOL). This note describes how the building is cooled and who
looks after it.

## 1. Zone map
- Zone A: production lines TOL-A, TOL-C and TOL-E. Served by rooftop unit AC-1.
- Zone B: production lines TOL-B and TOL-D. Served by rooftop unit AC-2.
- The warehouse and offices have separate units and are not covered here.

## 2. Target conditions
- Target air temperature on the production floor: 22 to 25 C.
- Humidity target: 40 to 60% relative humidity.
- Molding machine drive motors are specified for ambient limits stated in their own manuals.
  Check the motor's manual. Lines TOL-B and TOL-D run Voltrix VX-15 motors.

## 3. Rooftop units
- AC-1 and AC-2 are each a 30 ton rooftop packaged unit with two compressors.
- Filters are changed every quarter by the facilities team.
- Zone air temperature is not logged at Toledo; use a handheld reading when it matters.

## 4. Who to call
- Facilities supervisor: extension 310, for any HVAC fault or work order status.
- After hours: facilities on-call phone, listed at the security desk.
- HVAC contractor: Midwest Climate Service, for compressor and refrigerant work.
- Maintenance lead: extension 325, for motor and machine questions.

## 5. Work orders
- Facility work orders (FWO numbers) cover the building: HVAC, roof, lighting, compressed air, forklift chargers.
- Machine maintenance records (MR numbers) cover production assets. They are separate systems.

## 6. Temporary measures
- Portable spot coolers can be requested from facilities for a specific machine.
- Open dock doors are not an acceptable cooling measure because of dust and humidity.

## 7. Seasonal notes
- Lake effect: summer humidity is often above 60%; dehumidification runs on both units.
- Winter: heat is provided by gas unit heaters; zone targets are the same.

## 8. Escalation
- If a zone is above 28 C for more than four hours, tell the facilities supervisor and the maintenance lead.
- If a motor alarm coincides with a zone temperature problem, record both in the shift log.
