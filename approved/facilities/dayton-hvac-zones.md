# Dayton Facility: HVAC Zones

Skyline Plastics, Dayton OH (plant id PLT-DAY). This note describes how the building is cooled and who
looks after it.

## 1. Zone map
- Zone A: production lines DAY-A and DAY-D. Served by rooftop unit AC-1.
- Zone B: production lines DAY-B and DAY-C. Served by rooftop unit AC-2.
- The warehouse and offices have separate units and are not covered here.

## 2. Target conditions
- Target air temperature on the production floor: 22 to 25 C.
- Humidity target: 40 to 60% relative humidity.
- Molding machine drive motors are specified for ambient limits stated in their own manuals.
  Check the motor's manual.

## 3. Rooftop units
- AC-1 and AC-2 are each a 25 ton rooftop packaged unit with two compressors.
- Filters are changed every quarter by the facilities team.
- Units report fault codes to the facility log; work orders are raised in the facility work order system.

## 4. Who to call
- Facilities supervisor: extension 214, for any HVAC fault or work order status.
- After hours: facilities on-call phone, listed at the security desk.
- HVAC contractor: Midwest Climate Service, for compressor and refrigerant work. Parts lead time varies.
- Maintenance lead: extension 231, for motor and machine questions.

## 5. Work orders
- Facility work orders (FWO numbers) cover the building: HVAC, roof, lighting, compressed air, forklift chargers.
- Machine maintenance records (MR numbers) cover production assets. They are separate systems.
- A high priority HVAC work order should be reviewed by the facilities supervisor every week.

## 6. Temporary measures
- Portable spot coolers can be requested from facilities for a specific machine.
- Open dock doors are not an acceptable cooling measure because of dust and humidity.

## 7. Seasonal notes
- Summer load: both rooftop units run near capacity on days above 32 C outside.
- Spring and autumn: economizer mode is enabled when outside air is below 18 C.
- Winter: heat is provided by the building boiler loop; zone targets are the same.

## 8. Escalation
- If a zone is above 28 C for more than four hours, tell the facilities supervisor and the maintenance lead.
- If a motor alarm coincides with a zone temperature problem, record both in the shift log.
