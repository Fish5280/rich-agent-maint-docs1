# AcmeDrive AD-15K Screw Drive Motor - Service Manual

Document AD15K-SM-11. Applies to AcmeDrive AD-15K three-phase induction motors fitted to injection molding
machines (part number IMM-DRV-15KW).

## 1. Specifications
- Rated power: 15 kW
- Rated speed: 1800 rpm at 60 Hz
- Insulation class: F
- Enclosure: IP55, totally enclosed fan cooled (TEFC)
- Supply: 460 V, 3 phase, 60 Hz
- Bearings: 6310-2RS drive end, 6308-2RS non-drive end (sealed, regreasable)
- Mass: 112 kg

## 2. Temperature limits
- Normal running winding temperature: 55 to 80 C at rated load.
- Alarm at 100 C winding temp. The drive raises a warning.
- Trip at 120 C. The thermal protector opens the contactor. Reset after cooling below 70 C.
- Sustained operation above 90 C should be investigated even without an alarm.

## 3. Ambient conditions
- Rated for ambient up to 40 C.
- Derate 2% per C above 40 C.
- Keep the fan cowl and the surrounding 300 mm free of obstructions.
- Humidity up to 95% non-condensing.

## 4. Lubrication
- Lubrication: every 120 days or 3,000 operating hours, whichever first.
- Use grease AcmeDrive AG-3 (cartridge AD-GC400) or an NLGI 2 lithium complex grease.
- Quantity: 30 g drive end, 20 g non-drive end, applied with the motor running at low speed.
- Record each lubrication in the maintenance system with the date and technician.

## 5. Inspection
- Quarterly: check terminal torque, fan cowl, mounting bolts, and vibration level.
- Annual: insulation resistance test (minimum 100 megohm at 500 V DC).

## 6. Bearing wear symptoms
- A rough sound at the drive end.
- Slight speed fluctuation, more than 20 rpm under constant load.
- Bearing housing temperature more than 20 C above the frame.
- Replace with bearing kit AD15-BK.

## 7. Common faults
- Thermal trip: check cooling, ambient, lubrication and load before resetting.
- Encoder fault: check the cable and the connector.
- Rotor imbalance: vibration above 5.0 mm/s at the frame.
- Winding insulation failure: megohm reading below 10 megohm.

## 8. Spare parts
- Bearing kit AD15-BK, encoder AD15-EN, fan cowl AD15-FC, grease cartridge AD-GC400.
- Typical supplier lead time for the bearing kit is 1 to 2 weeks.
