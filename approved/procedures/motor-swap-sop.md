# SOP: Swapping a Screw Drive Motor (VX-15 and AD-15K)

Procedure SOP-MNT-021, revision 1. Applies to injection molding machines fitted with part number
IMM-DRV-15KW. Owner: Maintenance lead. Approval: Plant manager for any change of make.

## 1. Interchangeability
- Voltrix VX-15 and AcmeDrive AD-15K are both approved for part number IMM-DRV-15KW: 15 kW, 1800 rpm,
  460 V, same flange and shaft.
- Differences to plan for:
  - Mass: VX-15 118 kg, AD-15K 112 kg.
  - Rated ambient: VX-15 30 C, AD-15K 40 C.
  - Lubrication: VX-15 every 90 days with Voltrix VXG-2 only; AD-15K every 120 days with AG-3.
  - Alarm and trip: VX-15 95 C / 110 C; AD-15K 100 C / 120 C (drive parameters must be changed).
  - Bearing kits: VX15-BK and AD15-BK are not interchangeable.

## 2. When to swap
- A motor that has failed its insulation test (below 10 megohm).
- A motor taken out of service after repeat thermal trips (SOP-MNT-014 section 6).
- A planned change of make approved by the plant manager.

## 3. Before the swap
1. Confirm a spare motor is available at the plant or reserved at a sister plant.
2. Book a crane and two technicians; plan 4 hours per machine.
3. Agree the downtime slot with the production planner.
4. Prepare the drive parameter file for the new make.

## 4. Steps
1. Lockout/tagout (SOP-SAF-002).
2. Disconnect the supply and the encoder; mark the phase order.
3. Remove the coupling guard and the coupling.
4. Lift out the old motor with the crane.
5. Fit the new motor, align the coupling to within 0.05 mm.
6. Reconnect with the same phase order; connect the encoder.
7. Load the drive parameters for the new make (alarm, trip, rated current).
8. Remove locks, run at low speed, check direction, vibration and temperature.

## 5. After the swap
- Update the asset record: make, model, serial, install date.
- Set up the lubrication schedule for the new make.
- Move the right grease and bearing kit to the line's spares shelf.
- Return the removed motor to stores with a failure tag.

## 6. Records
- Corrective record "motor swap" with both serial numbers.
- The plant manager's approval is attached for a change of make.
