# SOP: Lockout/Tagout for Molding Machine Drive Motors

Procedure SOP-SAF-002, revision 5. Applies to all Skyline Plastics plants. Owner: Plant safety coordinator.
This is a safety-critical procedure. Steps are never skipped and the related preventive tasks are never
deferred (see PM-007 section 2).

## 1. When lockout is required
- Any work inside the terminal box, the fan cowl or the coupling guard.
- Bearing replacement, encoder replacement, motor swap.
- Resetting a thermal protector after opening any cover.
- Lubrication does not need lockout when done through the grease nipple with the guards fitted.

## 2. Energy sources on a molding machine drive
- Electrical: 460 V 3 phase supply to the drive, 24 V control.
- Stored: the drive's DC bus capacitors (wait 5 minutes after isolation).
- Mechanical: the screw can rotate under residual melt pressure; the hydraulic system must be at zero.
- Thermal: barrel heaters and the motor frame can be above 60 C.

## 3. Steps
1. Tell the line supervisor and the operators that the machine is being locked out.
2. Stop the machine with the normal stop.
3. Open the main isolator for the drive and fit your personal lock and tag.
4. Bleed the hydraulic pressure to zero and lock the valve.
5. Wait 5 minutes for the DC bus to discharge.
6. Try to start the machine from the operator panel (try-out). Nothing must move.
7. Test for absence of voltage at the motor terminals with an approved tester.

## 4. Group lockout
- Each person working on the machine fits their own lock.
- The lockout box is used when more than two people are involved.

## 5. Removing locks
- Only the person who fitted a lock removes it.
- Before removing: tools cleared, guards and covers refitted, people clear of the machine.
- Tell the line supervisor that the machine is returned.

## 6. Abandoned locks
- A lock left by an absent person is removed only by the plant safety coordinator, following the abandoned
  lock form, after trying to reach the person.

## 7. Training
- Every technician is trained before first lockout and refreshed every year.
- Contractors (for example the HVAC contractor) follow this SOP when working near machines.

## 8. Records
- The lockout log at each line records date, machine, people, locks and the reason.
- Logs are kept for three years.
