# SOP: Drive Motor Lubrication

Procedure SOP-MNT-009, revision 3. Applies to screw drive motors (part number IMM-DRV-15KW) at all
Skyline Plastics plants. Owner: Maintenance lead.

## 1. Intervals by make
| Make and model | Interval | Grease |
|---|---|---|
| Voltrix VX-15 | every 90 days or 2,000 operating hours, whichever first | Voltrix VXG-2 only |
| AcmeDrive AD-15K | every 120 days or 3,000 operating hours, whichever first | AcmeDrive AG-3 (cartridge AD-GC400) or NLGI 2 lithium complex |

- The motor's own manual is the authority. If this table and the manual ever differ, follow the manual and
  tell the maintenance lead.
- Intervals are not extended for light duty.
- On multi-shift lines the hour limit usually comes first; check the run hours, not only the calendar.

## 2. Grease rules
- Never mix greases. Voltrix VX-15 motors take Voltrix VXG-2 only; mixing can separate the thickener.
- Keep VX-15 and AD-15K cartridges on separate, labelled shelves.
- A cartridge with no label is not used.

## 3. Quantities
- Voltrix VX-15: 35 g drive end, 25 g non-drive end.
- AcmeDrive AD-15K: 30 g drive end, 20 g non-drive end.
- Apply with the motor running at low speed, through the grease nipple, with the relief plug open.
- Run for 15 minutes before refitting the relief plug.

## 4. Steps
1. Check the work order and the motor id on the nameplate.
2. Agree a slot with the line supervisor (low speed running is needed).
3. Clean the grease nipple and the relief plug area.
4. Apply the quantity above with a calibrated grease gun.
5. Check the purged grease: dark or gritty grease means bearing wear; note it and raise a corrective record.
6. Record the lubrication.

## 5. Records
- Record each lubrication in the maintenance system: motor id, date, technician, grease used, quantity.
- Record type "preventive", task "lubrication".
- A lubrication that cannot be done on its date follows the PM Deferral Policy (PM-007): reschedule within
  14 days, with the maintenance lead's approval recorded.

## 6. Signs that lubrication was missed
- Rising winding temperature at the same load.
- Speed fluctuation under constant load (VX-15: dips of 60 to 120 rpm).
- Rough sound at the drive end.
