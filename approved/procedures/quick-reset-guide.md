# Quick Reset Guide for Drive Motor Trips

For operators on night shift. Keeps the line running.

## When the drive shows a thermal trip
- Reset immediately at the drive, no cool-down needed for the first trip.
- If it trips again within the hour, call the on-call technician.

## Why
- Most first trips are nuisance trips from a warm afternoon.
- Stopping the line costs more than a reset.

## Records
- Note it in the shift log if you have time.
