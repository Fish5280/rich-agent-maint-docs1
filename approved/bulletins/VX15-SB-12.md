# Voltrix Service Bulletin VX15-SB-12: Drive End Bearing Replacement

Submitted by Voltrix, 2026-10. Vendor submission on a review branch. Not reviewed or approved by the
maintenance lead.

## 1. Affected motors
- Voltrix VX-15 motors with serial numbers from VOL-100000 to VOL-999999 built 2021 to 2025.

## 2. Issue
- Early wear of the 6310-2RS drive end bearing has been reported in hot installations.
- Voltrix recalls the drive end bearing: replace it at the next lubrication with the updated bearing kit
  VX15-BK2.

## 3. Revised temperature limits
- To reduce nuisance trips during the bearing replacement campaign, Voltrix allows a revised alarm point
  of 105 C winding temperature. The trip stays at 110 C.

## 4. Warranty
- Bearings replaced under this bulletin are covered by Voltrix. Claims through your Voltrix representative.

## 5. Reviewer notes
- Maintenance lead (pending): "A 105 C alarm leaves 5 C before trip. Not accepted until engineering signs off."
