# Spare Parts Policy for Drive Motors

Policy SP-003, applies to all Skyline Plastics plants in the Great Lakes territory. Owner: Maintenance lead.
Purchasing contact: the territory purchasing team.

## 1. Approved supplier
- Drive motor spares are bought from MotorParts Direct (category: spare parts).
- Quoted supplier lead times are a planning guide; the delivery date on the order is what counts.

## 2. Lead times to plan with
| Part | Code | Typical lead time |
|---|---|---|
| Voltrix VX-15 bearing kit | VX15-BK | 3 to 6 weeks |
| AcmeDrive AD-15K bearing kit | AD15-BK | 1 to 2 weeks |
| Voltrix VX-15 encoder | VX15-EN | 3 to 4 weeks |
| AcmeDrive AD-15K encoder | AD15-EN | 1 to 2 weeks |
| Voltrix grease cartridge VXG-2 (400 g) | VXG-2 | 1 week |
| AcmeDrive grease cartridge | AD-GC400 | 1 week |

## 3. Minimum stock per plant
- One bearing kit per make in use at the plant.
- Two grease cartridges per make in use.
- One complete spare motor per territory, held at the plant with the most motors of that part number,
  available to sister plants.

## 4. Reordering
- Reorder when stock falls to the minimum. Do not wait for a failure.
- A backordered part is reported to the maintenance lead the same week, with the supplier's note.
- If a bearing kit is backordered and a motor shows bearing wear symptoms, the maintenance lead checks the
  sister plants' stock and the spare motor before production is put at risk.

## 5. Reserving and moving parts between plants
- Parts can be moved between plants with the agreement of both maintenance leads.
- A reserved part is marked in the material supply record with the receiving plant.
- Only the maintenance lead or the planner reserves a spare motor.

## 6. Receiving
- Check the part code and quantity against the order.
- Grease cartridges are checked for the correct make and stored on the labelled shelf.
- Record the receipt in the material supply record.

## 7. Records
- Material supply records keep the order date, due date, status and receipt date.
- Records are kept for seven years.
