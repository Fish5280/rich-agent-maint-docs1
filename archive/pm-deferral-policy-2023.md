# Preventive Maintenance Deferral Policy (2023 edition, WITHDRAWN)

Policy PM-007, 2023 edition. Withdrawn and replaced. Kept for audit reference only.

## 1. Purpose
Sets how preventive maintenance can be moved when production needs the machine.

## 2. When a deferral is allowed
- A PM may be deferred for any production need.
- The deferral should be noted in the maintenance system.

## 3. Rescheduling
- Deferred preventive maintenance must be rescheduled within 45 days.
- If the new date slips, a further 45 days may be added once.

## 4. Approval
- Deferrals are approved by the production supervisor of the line.
- The maintenance lead is informed at the monthly review.

## 5. Escalation
- A PM more than 90 days past its original date is reported to the plant manager.

## 6. Records
- Records are kept for five years.
