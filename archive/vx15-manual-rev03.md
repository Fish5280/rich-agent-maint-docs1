# Voltrix VX-15 Screw Drive Motor - Service Manual (rev 03, SUPERSEDED)

Document VX15-SM-03. Superseded by VX15-SM-04. Kept for reference only.

## 1. Specifications
- Rated power: 15 kW
- Rated speed: 1800 rpm at 60 Hz
- Insulation class: F
- Enclosure: IP55, TEFC
- Supply: 460 V, 3 phase, 60 Hz

## 2. Temperature limits
- Normal running winding temperature: 60 to 90 C at rated load.
- Alarm threshold: 100 C winding temperature.
- Trip threshold: 120 C. Reset after the motor has cooled.
- (Rev 04 lowered both limits after field failures of the winding insulation.)

## 3. Ambient conditions
- Rated for ambient up to 30 C.

## 4. Lubrication
- Lubrication: every 90 days.
- Use grease Voltrix VXG-1.

## 5. Bearing wear symptoms
- A rough sound at the drive end.
- Speed fluctuation under constant load.

## 6. Spare parts
- Bearing kit VX15-BK1 (discontinued), encoder VX15-EN.
